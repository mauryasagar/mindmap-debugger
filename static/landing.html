Where it breaks down

  • A meeting transcript says the deadline is fixed. Ten minutes later, someone says there isn't time to hit it.
  • Nobody flags it — everyone was listening for their own part, not tracking the whole argument.
  • The same thing happens in design docs, contracts, and long debates: claims pile up faster than any one reader can cross-check them.

WHAT MINDMAP DEBUGGER CATCHES

  • CIRCULAR A chain of claims that loops back and uses its own conclusion as support.
